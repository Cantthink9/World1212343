Unyielding Seas

You know how the waves were made?
By Fire and Rain
The flame craved the grand white plane, making the seeds for 300 islands
The Rain came then the caved land, thus turning the craved land into grand Sea
And the Rain brought it's Prince who renamed himself Comet, for his present brought great joy, but he never stayed for long
Comet had a Soul Edition, which translates to English as "To talk to tens."
But that was made in the Mince War. And once in a blue moon, the edition was weaker than it is now. He could "talk" to millions.
And He was prolific with kids, having 312 kids in the Grand Sea alone.
Most of those kids had the Soul Edition, which allowed them to share important information as if talking face-to-face, no matter how far apart the islands were.
